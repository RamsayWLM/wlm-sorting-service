WLM SORTING SERVICE
====================

This app lets White Lights Media remotely sort and organize your photos —
you install it, pick a folder, and our team does the rest over a secure
connection. You never need to use the sorting tool yourself.


STEP 1 — Opening the app for the first time
--------------------------------------------
macOS will likely block it the first time with a message like:

    "WLM Sorting Service" cannot be opened because the developer
    cannot be verified.

This is expected — the app isn't distributed through the Mac App Store,
so macOS is just being cautious. To open it anyway:

    1. Right-click (or Control-click) the app icon
    2. Choose "Open" from the menu
    3. Click "Open" again in the popup that appears

You only need to do this once — it opens normally from then on.


STEP 2 — Follow the app's own setup screen
--------------------------------------------
Once it opens, the app walks you through everything itself: installing
Tailscale (the secure connection this runs over), signing in with your
own free account, and choosing a folder for us to work in. Just follow
it step by step — nothing to figure out on your own.

One thing worth knowing in advance: during the Tailscale install, macOS
will ask permission to add a VPN/network extension — click Allow. That's
Tailscale's own setup, not this app, and it's normal.

When it's done, you'll see a green dot and "Connected — Ready for White
Lights Media." That's it — you're set up, and there's nothing else to
send us or do.


QUESTIONS OR SOMETHING NOT WORKING?
--------------------------------------------
Get in touch with Ramsay at White Lights Media. If something looks wrong,
there's a "Copy diagnostics..." button on the app's status screen — copy
that and send it along, it helps us see what's going on without needing
to ask you a bunch of questions first.
